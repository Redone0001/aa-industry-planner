Batch & character defaults

Reaction limits cap each job. A smaller final batch produces only the runs still needed; materials, time and installation fees use those actual runs. The duration limit overrides the run-count limit. Time multipliers represent your effective skill and implant bonuses, excluding facility and blueprint bonuses. Enter rates as fractions: 0.8 = 80%, 0.0025 = 0.25%.
